# Constellations mode

Layer plugin. Draws the 88 constellations and the Pleiades. Locking on to any star of a figure targets the whole figure.

`ConstellationScreen` is a modal with Overview, Stars and Mythology tabs. Mythology picks a culture, shows its story and source, and redraws the figure with that culture's lines where they are bundled. Ten figures have stories. Stories live in `src/data/mythology.ts`.

The figure is drawn with running stitches in the Needlepoint look.
