# Review and status, 2026-11-26

A closing review at the end of the first phase of development, after satellites brought the Layers mode back and Maestro flows were added. The phase is tagged `v0.1.0`. The changes from this pass landed in one commit.

## Status

- **Backlog:** every feature step is done. What is left is the device pass ([task 8](../backlog/device.md)), which needs a phone and a person, and the ideas under **Later**, which wait for a new phase.
- **Build:** `main` is clean and in sync with GitHub, and CI passes. `npm run check` runs types, lint, formatting, the docs check and 210 tests in 26 suites.
- **Size:** about 6,300 lines of app TypeScript outside `src/data`, 940 lines of unit tests, five Maestro flows, 19 ADRs, 29 commits.
- **Verified vs not:** see the [handoff](../handoff.md). In short, everything the simulator can show has been checked, and the main flows are repeatable with `npm run test:e2e`. The camera, sensors, haptics, permission prompts, satellite passes against the real sky and anything on a physical phone have not.

## Since the last review

The 2026-11-25 review made five recommendations:

1. **Satellite passes, then show Layers again:** done. Bright satellites led by the ISS move with time and time travel, with details, the next visible pass, search and find in sky. Orbits are fetched from CelesTrak at launch, with a cache and a bundled fallback (ADR 18).
2. **GitHub description and topics:** done.
3. **Task 8 on a phone:** not started, by design. It is the first thing to do when a phone is at hand, and does not block the tag (see below).
4. **Maestro (task 3):** done. Five flows run on the simulator: launch, Polaris from search to the logbook, every look, night vision and details (ADR 19). They passed twice in a row. CI does not run them, since they need a booted simulator and a development build.
5. **Trim the looks:** kept for now. All four looks stay, and reducing them is left to a later phase. The duplicate title on the constellation screen was fixed in an earlier pass.

## Decisions at the end of the phase

- **Tag `v0.1.0` now.** The tag marks the end of the phase, not a phone-verified release. ADR 11 and the device task are updated to match. A release after the device pass gets the next version.
- **Keep all four looks.** Default, Starship, Glass & chrome and Needlepoint stay. Each new control still needs a check in every look (ADR 14); a reduction is expected later.

## Risks carried into the next phase

- **No phone verification.** This is still the largest risk. Pointing a phone at the sky depends on sensors, true north and the camera's field of view, none of them tried on a phone.
- **Looks multiply checks.** Four looks make every interface change cost more; Glass & chrome depends on iOS 26.
- **Content review.** Stories cover 10 figures and need community review before a store release. The reuse terms of arXiv and the NASA Exoplanet Archive are unchecked.
- **Photo capture** is unbuilt. Identify saves a data card with no photo.

## Recommendations for the next phase

1. **Run task 8 on a phone.** Start with sensors, the field of view and the camera, then an ISS pass.
2. **Decide on the looks** with the phone results in hand: those that read well over the camera stay.
3. **Photo capture**, once the camera is proven on a device.
