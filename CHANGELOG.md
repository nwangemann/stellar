# Changelog

## Unreleased (0.1.0)

- Scaffold, design system and app shell with a simulated sky.
- Reticle lock-on, info sheet and stub logbook.
- Runs on the iOS simulator. Not yet run on a physical device or Android.
- Licensed under MIT.
- Themed screen header and hidden status bar, so night vision stays red on iOS.
- Real stars and constellations from bundled data, placed for the current time and location.
- Star details with planets and papers, and a constellation modal with mythology by culture.
- The Capture button is now Identify.
- Added the CanvasKit licence notice.
- Selectable looks in Settings: Default, Starship, Glass & chrome and Needlepoint.
- Moon, Sun and planets in the sky, with distance, brightness, phase, rise and set.
- The sky follows the phone, with a setting to drag instead. Not yet tried on a phone.
- Compass alignment on a known object, a low accuracy notice, and a field of view that matches the camera.
- Time travel: a slider moves the sky from 12 hours back to 24 hours ahead.
- Search for stars, constellations, planets, the Sun and the Moon by name.
- Find in sky turns the view to an object from search or its details, or leads there with an arrow when the sky follows the phone.
- The star screen links to its constellation, and logbook rows open details.
- Details zoom out of their object in the sky, and back into it when closed.
- Fixed: Triangulum Australe is matched to its stars.
